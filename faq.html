Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents and families need to know about curated channels, parental controls, child accounts, Family Pass, and the Android app.

Safety & Basics What is FamiTube and how is it different from YouTube or YouTube Kids? expand_more
FamiTube is a 100% parent- and curator-approved video platform. Children only see channels and videos explicitly curated for their age tier—with zero algorithmic rabbit holes, no surprise autoplay, no public comments, and hybrid playback supporting sandboxed YouTube videos and direct MP4/HLS streams.
Safety & Basics Are curated videos on FamiTube really 100% free? expand_more
Yes. Every curated video on FamiTube is 100% free ($0 / Rp 0 forever) with zero video paywalls. FamiTube Family Pass is completely optional and only unlocks multi-child profiles, teen tiers, private family channels, and advanced household controls.
Safety & Basics Can children sign up or change parental rules on their own? expand_more
No. FamiTube has zero self-registration for kids. Only verified Parent accounts (via email/password or Google Sign-In) can create child profiles, set passcodes or PINs, and manage channels, timers, and safety defaults.
Channels & YouTube What is the difference between On-Demand Channels and 24H Live TV Channels? expand_more
On-Demand channels let kids pick approved episodes anytime. 24H Live TV channels broadcast a scheduled linear lineup (with default, weekday, and date-specific schedules supporting whole videos or clip ranges) for calm, lean-back viewing without decision fatigue.
Channels & YouTube How do Controlled YouTube Search and video requests work? expand_more
Controlled YouTube Search and video approval requests are available for Young Kids (5–8) and Older Kids (9–12+) when enabled by a parent—Toddler (2–4) profiles cannot search or send video approval requests. Parents receive a notification, can preview the requested video directly inside the Parent Dashboard, and approve or reject it. Free accounts include up to 3 requests per day, while Family Pass offers unlimited requests and optional instant approval.
Channels & YouTube How do @YouTube channel subscriptions and the 3 trust levels work? expand_more
Families can subscribe to YouTube channels by @handle, browse uploads and channel playlists, and auto-refresh latest uploads when opening a channel. Parents assign per-child trust levels: Trusted (auto-approves channel videos), Supervised (requires per-video parent approval), or Blocked (hides and blocks the channel).
Channels & YouTube Can I create private family channels and invite co-parents, relatives, or teachers? expand_more
Yes, with Family Pass. You can set a channel's visibility to Private and generate invite links using permission presets (Viewer, Commenter, Suggester, Contributor, or custom permissions) with optional max-use and expiration limits.
Parental Controls How do Age Tiers, Safety Presets, and Tag Filters work? expand_more
FamiTube uses a 3-tier developmental ladder—Tier 1: Toddler TV (ages 1.5–3), Tier 2: Young Kids (ages 4–6), and Tier 3: Older Kids (ages 7+)—and parents always have full control over each child's tier (a birthday only suggests a starting tier during initial setup). Each tier tailors both topics and features, pairing with tag-driven categories and safety presets like Calm Mode, No Shorts, No Toy / Unboxing, Study & Focus Mode, and Bedtime Safe.
Kids & Games How does Tier 1 Toddler TV Mode work (linear channels, sticky playhead, and Nap Cooldown)? expand_more
Tier 1 Toddler TV Mode replaces on-demand thumbnail grids, search, scrub bars, and skip buttons with a fixed spatial dial of calm, slow-paced channels (English, Bahasa Indonesia, or non-verbal). Tuning in plays a gentle bumper and resumes where the child left off (Option B sticky playhead), with 1 loop = 1 session (default 15 minutes) ending on a calm wind-down video before entering a configurable Nap Cooldown (default 30 minutes) that rests all Toddler channels and Favorites until time is up or a parent enters their PIN to Wake Up TV.
Parental Controls How do Daily Screen Time Limits, Global vs. Age-Specific Controls, and the Parent PIN work? expand_more
A 4-digit Parent PIN protects settings and quick PIN-gated controls from Kid Mode. Global Controls (PIN, Family Language, Culture Focus, daily timers, and attention checks) apply across the household, while Parent Mode adapts per-child settings to each child's age tier—for example, Toddler profiles show loop length, Nap Cooldown, channel toggles, and Wake Up TV while hiding YouTube Search.
Parental Controls What are Parent Safety Reviews and Community Advisories? expand_more
Parents can rate curated videos as Recommended or Caution with notes so other families can spot pacing, stimulation, or content sensitivity nuances at a glance before approving.
Kids & Games How do Favorites, Custom Library Lists, Educational Games, and Famibux work? expand_more
Kids can heart Favorite videos—with a dedicated Favorites heart tab right on the Toddler (2–4) bottom bar and saved under Profile—while Young Kids (5–8) and Older Kids (9–12+) can also organize approved videos into custom Library playlists (playlists and add-to-playlist are disabled for Toddlers). In the Games tab on Android and App Preview, kids play age-locked brain games and interactive H5P learning activities to earn Famibux and unlock level badges.
Kids & Games How do Flip-Page Storybooks and Mic-Locked Read-Along work? expand_more
FamiTube includes illustrated flip-page storybooks in English and Bahasa Indonesia (plus custom story authoring in Curator Studio). Toddlers (2–4) default to Story Flip mode with gentle page narration and an optional Read-Along toggle, while Young Kids (5–8) default to Mic Read-Along where the page only flips forward once every word on the page has been read aloud into the microphone. Kids can tap any word to hear its pronunciation, get a gentle helper boost after 3 tries on a tricky word, and earn Famibux upon finishing a story (Older Kids hide younger stories unless browsing all).
Plans & Vouchers What is included in FamiTube Free vs. FamiTube Family Pass? expand_more
FamiTube Free includes the full curated video library, 1 child profile, kids tiers (2–12), 1 active safety preset, 1 custom kid playlist, up to 3 YouTube search requests per day, and basic daily timer/watch history. Family Pass unlocks unlimited child profiles and playlists, Teen tiers (13–17), stacked presets plus custom tag rules, unlimited search requests, private family channels with invites, attention tracking, and weekly parent topic insights.
Plans & Vouchers How do Parent Referral Codes and Family Pass Vouchers work? expand_more
Every parent account has a unique referral code in Parent Mode. When another parent registers using your code, you receive a 3-month Family Pass voucher with no expiration date that you can activate whenever you are ready.
Apps & Devices Where can my family use FamiTube, and how can I preview the Android app? expand_more
FamiTube works on the web via FamiTube Web and Curator Studio, and natively on Android phones and tablets (Android 8.0+ / API 26+). Anyone can also test-drive Child Mode and Parent Side-by-Side Mode directly in the browser using the interactive Mobile App Preview.
